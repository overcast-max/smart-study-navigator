## About the Project

Smart Study Navigator is an intelligent educational assistant designed to solve two major student problems:
1. **Lack of study direction** - Students don't know what to study first or how to manage multiple subjects
2. **Lack of concentration** - Students get distracted without realizing it

## Key Features

- **Smart Study Roadmap** - AI-generated daily study plans based on exam dates, subjects, and topic strengths
- **Focus Detection System** - Real-time monitoring with instant alerts when distraction is detected
- **AI-Based Recommendations** - Personalized strategies, optimal duration, and smart revision timing
- **Progress Dashboard** - Visual analytics of completion, focus scores, streaks, and weak areas

## Technologies Used

## How to Use

1. Open the live demo link
2. Click "Start Your Journey" to begin setup
3. Enter your details (Name, Class, Exam, Date)
4. Select subjects and categorize topics as Weak/Strong
5. Generate your personalized Smart Roadmap
6. Explore the Dashboard with Focus Monitor, AI Recommendations, and Progress Analytics
